# Bounded originality review

Decision: **GO for this MVP**, with adjacent precedents explicitly acknowledged. This is not a claim of worldwide firstness or exhaustive ecosystem coverage.

Internal comparison reused the user-provided nine-mechanism baseline only; no old repository source, business mechanism or application architecture was copied. RuleGate, EvidenceCourt, PolicyDrift, ClauseMesh, EIM, MAM, Semantic Cycle Accord, ContextPass and SignalLedger concern decisions, evidence, compatibility, attestations, exchange or status. SPLICE's durable unit is a newly generated creative work with two contributor-owned inputs and a provenance graph. It does not expose a generic text acceptance endpoint or relation matrix.

External checks were limited to GenLayer collaborative creation, character fusion, generative genealogy/worlds and content consensus. Targeted searches used two search systems; no confirmed equivalent GenLayer two-author fusion primitive was found within that bounded search.

| Adjacent reference | Overlap | Material distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Progena](https://www.hackquest.io/projects/Progena) | Two parent agents, AI hybrid generation and onchain inheritance | Its published flow describes NFT breeding, an offchain daemon, compute synthesis, rootHash finalization and royalties. SPLICE stores a text creative object only after GenLayer Validators independently assess feature preservation and integration. No NFT, daemon, economy or autonomous agent runtime. This is the closest adjacent precedent; fusion/genealogy alone is not novel. |
| [Coop Tales](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/coop-tales-153g7) | Collaborative AI storytelling and shared lore | Interactive narrator/choice/story summarization flow, rather than two immutable registered seeds → a single grounded consensus-accepted child with fixed parent IDs. |
| [Scenario multi-character tooling](https://help.scenario.com/articles/8459982289-generate-multi-character-scenes) | Creative blending / character visual composition | Image generation workflow; no contribution registration, native onchain candidate acceptance or immutable lineage protocol in the referenced feature documentation. |
| [GenLayer official LLM examples](https://docs.genlayer.com/developers/intelligent-contracts/examples/llm-hello-world) | Leader generation and criteria-based acceptance | Infrastructure pattern, not the same creative contribution/permission/parent-bound generation/provenance state machine. |

The intended incremental capability is the conjunction of two authors' public inputs, native generated object, independent source-grounded content validation and append-only parent/contributor identity. It is reusable across creative remix products without adding transfer, payment, ranking or authenticity mechanisms.

Evidence limits: compared public descriptions and official documentation; did not perform a full ecosystem/source-code audit. No claim of guaranteed GenLayer acceptance or global originality is made. Ordinary creative generators and NFT breeding are acknowledged, not hidden.
