## Full description

The contract records immutable creative seeds with explicit public derivation permission. A fusion requires two depth-zero seeds from different wallet addresses. A GenLayer Leader proposes a bounded JSON creation, and Validators independently review whether it preserves a distinctive feature from each parent and integrates them into a causal mechanism. V2 rejects malformed or ambiguous model output, mechanically binds review quotations to the frozen source and candidate fields, and saves only after accepted consensus. Parent IDs, both creators, parent content hashes and the versioned input hash remain with the resulting work. Requests are sender-bound and idempotent.

On Stable Studionet, deployment, two seeds and one real AI fusion are FINALIZED. Work #3 is Prismatic Resonator Beetle, derived from Prism Beetle (#1) and Echo Loom (#2). T04 reached MAJORITY_AGREE with 3 AGREE, 1 DISAGREE and 1 IDLE in the final round. Its receipt reports zero network fee. A separate human review confirmed the visible semantic result; it is not an additional onchain consensus proof. Exact GenVM node binary version is unverified. Contribution addresses do not establish distinct human identity or legal IP ownership. The submitted scope has no token, payments, royalties or NFT marketplace.
